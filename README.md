🎓 Campus Find – Campus Lost & Found Portal
Campus Find is a web-based Lost and Found platform developed to make it easier for students to report and recover misplaced belongings within their college campus.
The platform provides a centralized space where students can post details about lost or found items, browse reported items, and connect with the people who may have found or lost their belongings.
✨ Key Features

Report lost items.
Post details of found items.
Browse and search for reported belongings.
Simple and user-friendly interface.
Helps improve communication among students.

🎯 Project Objective
To create a digital solution that simplifies the lost and found process in colleges, reduces the chances of permanently losing personal belongings, and promotes a helpful campus community.
